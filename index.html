Small tools for the parts of life that get complicated

I make iOS apps and web tools for studying, travel, training and research. Each one starts with a problem I have run into myself, or seen up close, and tries to solve it without adding another layer of noise.

Offline-first mobile tools for studying, travel and fitness.

TravelBudget

Forma

Workouts sized to your recovery

Web Tools

A browser-based tool for looking at companies with a little more structure.

MoatCheck

Research company moats from public filings

Review SEC filings, financial metrics and your notes against a moat checklist.

No account for most of the apps. They keep working offline where they can. The useful parts are not hidden behind a subscription.

I prefer software that is easy to understand, quiet when you are not using it, and respectful of the information you put into it.
