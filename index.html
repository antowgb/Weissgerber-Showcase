iOS apps built for real use, not just downloads

Three focused tools, each solving one problem well: deep focus, travel spending, and training recovery. Built solo, designed to earn a permanent place on your home screen: no accounts, no tracking, nothing running in the background you didn't ask for.

Vitae

Binaural beats for deep focus

Two frequencies, one state of concentration. A study companion with a built-in timer.

TravelBudget

Every currency, one clear balance

Track spending across countries without creating an account or opening a spreadsheet.

Forma

Workouts sized to your recovery

A daily session generated from what's actually recovered, not a fixed weekly script.

No account to create. No connection required. No subscription forced on you.

The same principle runs through all three apps: build the smallest thing that actually works, and let it work offline.
