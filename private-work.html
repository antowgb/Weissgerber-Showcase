Private work

Alongside the public products, I build internal systems for teams under private engagements. This isn't a product for sale, it's a short, generic overview of the kind of work involved.

What this work looks like

Most private engagements sit outside the public product line entirely. They're internal tools, built for a specific team's workflow rather than for general distribution, and they're not linked or demoed publicly. The shape of the work tends to fall into a few recurring categories.

Research automation

Systems that gather and structure information from multiple sources so a team can review it faster, instead of doing the same repetitive lookups by hand.

Data enrichment pipelines

Pipelines that take a sparse dataset and fill in the gaps from reliable sources, with validation steps to keep the output trustworthy.

Content generation with human validation

Drafting or summarization tools where a person always reviews the output before it's used, so automation speeds up the work without removing judgment from the loop.

Workflow tooling

Small internal tools that remove a specific manual step from a team's day-to-day process: a form, a dashboard, a script that used to be a spreadsheet.

Why it's not listed as a product

This kind of work is built for one team's specific context, under a private agreement, and isn't meant for general use. It doesn't get a public page, screenshots, or a store listing, because it isn't something anyone else could install or subscribe to. This page exists only to describe the type of work, not to showcase a specific client or deliverable.
