The mechanism behind it

Each ear receives a slightly different frequency through your headphones, for example 100 Hz in the left ear, 108 Hz in the right. Your brain can't reconcile the two directly, so it constructs a third, perceived tone from the 8 Hz gap between them. Sustained exposure nudges your brainwaves toward that rhythm, a state suited to studying or relaxing. It isn't a metaphor; it's how the auditory system processes phase difference between two ears.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are binaural beats?

Binaural beats happen when you listen to two slightly different frequencies, one in each ear, through headphones. Your brain perceives a third tone from the difference between them. That perceived tone is the binaural beat.

Do binaural beats actually work?

Binaural beats work as part of a focus routine for many people, similar to how ambient noise or music helps concentration. Research findings vary by study design, and we don't claim they're a medical treatment for any condition. What we do claim: a consistent pre-study ritual, structured sessions, and a distraction-free environment measurably help people start and stay focused.

Do I need headphones for binaural beats to work?

Yes. Binaural beats rely on each ear receiving a different frequency. Without headphones, both ears hear the same mixed signal and the effect does not occur.

What's the difference between alpha and theta waves in Vitae?

Alpha frequencies are generally associated with a calm, alert state, useful for steady focus and routine work. Theta frequencies are associated with a deeper, more relaxed state, often used for extended study sessions or memory focused work. Vitae lets you choose based on what you are doing.

How long should I listen in one session?

There is no fixed rule. Many users listen for the length of a study or work session, often paired with the built in Pomodoro timer, anywhere from 25 minutes to a couple of hours.

Can binaural beats help with ADHD or trouble focusing?

Some people with attention difficulties report that binaural beats or similar background audio help them settle into a task. This is anecdotal rather than a clinical claim. Vitae is not a medical device and does not treat ADHD or any other condition.

Does Vitae work without an internet connection?

Yes. All audio is generated and stored locally on your device once the app is installed. No connection is required to use it.

Is Vitae safe to use every day?

Binaural beats through headphones at a reasonable, comfortable volume are generally considered safe for daily use. As with any headphone use, avoid high volumes for extended periods.
