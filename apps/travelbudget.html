Built for the way spending actually happens on the road

Prices arrive in yen, euros, pesos, sometimes three currencies in the same afternoon. TravelBudget keeps a single running total in your home currency, updated the moment you log a purchase: no manual conversion, no spreadsheet, no account to set up first.

Getting started

1

Set your trip budget

Choose a total and your home currency. Everything else is tracked against it automatically.

2

Log expenses as you go

Category, amount, local currency: logged in a few seconds, converted instantly.

3

Watch the balance move

Remaining budget, daily pace, and category breakdowns update the moment you add an entry.

What's inside

Multi-currency by default

Log an expense in any currency; it converts to your home currency automatically.

Real-time balance

Remaining budget and daily spending rate update instantly with every entry.

Category breakdowns

See spending by food, transport, lodging, and activities to spot where the budget is going.

Trip templates

Start from presets for Japan, a European road trip, or Southeast Asia with suggested categories.

Receipt capture

Photograph receipts for reference. Everything stays stored locally on your device.

Fully offline

Works with no signal, at the border, or mid-flight. No cloud sync required.
