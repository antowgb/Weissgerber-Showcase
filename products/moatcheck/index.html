Frequently Asked Questions

What is a company moat?

A moat is a sustainable competitive advantage that protects a company from competitors. Common moats include brand strength, network effects, cost advantages, switching costs, and intangible assets like patents. The term was popularized by Warren Buffett.

Where does MoatCheck get its data?

MoatCheck uses publicly available data sources including SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q reports), financial data providers, and RSS feeds for news. All data comes from public sources.

Is MoatCheck investment advice?

No. MoatCheck is a research tool that helps you organize and analyze public information. It does not provide investment recommendations, and you should always do your own research and consult with qualified professionals before making investment decisions.

Do I need an account to use MoatCheck?

No. MoatCheck does not require an account. Your preferences and recent searches are stored locally in your browser.

How accurate is the data?

Data is sourced from official SEC filings and reputable financial data providers. However, you should always verify important information against primary sources before making decisions.
