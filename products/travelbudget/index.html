Frequently Asked Questions

How do I estimate a travel budget before a trip?

A common approach is to break your trip into categories, accommodation, food, transport, activities, and estimate a daily cost per category based on your destination and travel style. TravelBudget's templates give you a starting point per destination that you can adjust based on your own plans.

Do I need to create an account to use TravelBudget?

No. TravelBudget works fully without an account. All your trip and expense data stays on your device.

How does multi-currency tracking work?

When you log an expense in a foreign currency, TravelBudget converts it automatically into your chosen reference currency using cached exchange rates, so your running total always reflects a single, consistent view of your budget.

Does TravelBudget work without an internet connection abroad?

Yes. Exchange rates are cached the last time you had a connection, and all core features, logging expenses, viewing your budget, work fully offline.

Can I split an expense between multiple travelers?

Yes, you can add participants to an expense and split it evenly or by custom amounts, without any of them needing an account.

Is my financial data stored in the cloud?

No. TravelBudget stores everything locally on your device. There is no account, sync, or cloud backend involved.

What if my trip has no fixed end date?

You can still track a budget without an end date, though features based on daily averages will adjust or hide depending on whether an end date is set.
