About Weissgerber

I'm Antonin Weissgerber, an independent developer building digital products that respect your privacy and your time.

Philosophy

I like software that does its job clearly and then gets out of the way. Most of my products start with one specific annoyance, so I try not to turn them into something larger than the problem requires.

Privacy first

Your data is yours. I don't collect unnecessary analytics, track behavior across apps, or sync information to the cloud unless you explicitly choose to. Most products run entirely offline or with minimal network dependencies.

Simplicity over features

I resist adding every possible feature. Each product does one thing well rather than many things adequately, which keeps it fast, reliable, and easy to use.

Sustainable pricing

No dark patterns, no attention-hijacking notifications. Products are either free or offer a one-time purchase for lifetime access. A recurring subscription only appears when that's genuinely the better model.

Quality and craft

Each product is built solo, taking the time to get the details right: performance, accessibility, and a smooth, thoughtful experience.

Background

I've spent several years making iOS apps and web tools for productivity, travel, fitness and investment research. The products come from problems I have had myself, or conversations with people who were trying to do something the hard way.

That might mean planning revision, keeping track of spending abroad, deciding what to train today, or making company research less scattered. The subject changes, but I keep coming back to the same idea: make the next step easier to see.
