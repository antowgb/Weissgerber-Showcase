About me

I'm an independent iOS developer. I build apps that respect your privacy and your time.

My philosophy

Great software doesn't need to be complicated or invasive. Each app I ship solves one problem, no more and no less. Four ideas guide the work.

Privacy first

Your data is yours. I don't collect analytics, track behavior, or sync information to the cloud unless you explicitly choose to. Most of my apps run entirely offline.

Simplicity over features

I resist adding every possible feature. Each app does one thing well rather than many things adequately, which keeps it fast, reliable, and easy to use.

Sustainable pricing

No dark patterns, no attention-hijacking notifications. Apps are either free or offer a one-time purchase for lifetime access. A recurring subscription only appears when that's genuinely the better model.

Quality and craft

Each app is built solo, taking the time to get the details right: performance, accessibility, and a smooth, thoughtful experience.

Background

I've spent several years developing iOS apps across productivity, travel, and fitness. Each one reflects a real problem I or people I know have faced, along with a commitment to solving it without compromise.

Whether it's helping students focus during exams, letting travelers track expenses across currencies, or giving lifters smarter training guidance, the goal is the same: tools that quietly improve daily life.
